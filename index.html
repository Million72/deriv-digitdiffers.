Trading Configuration

Differs · 1 tick

The bot watches every selected market tick by tick. A market's lowest digit is the digit from 1 to 8 with the lowest percentage in the analysis window (0 and 9 are never used). When that lowest digit is below the trigger (default 8.5%) and its percentage has just decreased by at least the minimum (default 0.1%) on that tick, the bot instantly buys one Differs on it. If there is no decrease, there is no trade. In-and-out rule: at most 1 to 4 back-to-back trades on one market, then that market is paused. Differs wins about 90% of the time but pays only a small amount, so one loss costs far more than a win earns. Martingale can rapidly increase risk. Test on a demo account first.
