PAPER VALIDATION

Martingale: disabled.

Martingale is optional (off by default). When enabled, it scales both LIVE and PAPER stakes so paper results reflect the staked strategy. Each mode maintains its own cycle and is budgeted against its own session P/L. No forced entries. Only markets with 4/4 window agreement are traded. The bot first requests a live proposal, records its actual payout, and observes the next tick in paper mode. Automatic buying remains blocked for each market until its paper results show a positive 95% lower confidence bound on mean net return. A positive historical result still does not guarantee future profitability.
